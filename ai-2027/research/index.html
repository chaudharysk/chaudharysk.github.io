Research papers

Pick a paper that helps with a question in your project. Look at how the authors tested their claim.

Reading list reviewed 19 November 2026

Write three short paper notes on different topics. Pick one paper to examine in more detail and, if possible, try a small experiment. This can be part of the final project. The plan already includes about 25–40 hours for this work; do not add those hours again.

Papers and reports to choose from

  • Long-running agents

    Anthropic: Harness design for long-running application development, 24 March 2026; engineering report

    What does checkpointing, decomposition or an evaluator add beyond the model itself?

  • External memory

    Anthropic: The components of agent memory, 6 November 2026; ReasoningBank, research paper revised March 2026

    When do searchable logs or selected notes help? What is the cost of creating and maintaining them?

  • Reasoning and test-time computation

    DeepSeek-R1, revised January 2026; the HF reasoning material

    Separate learning during training from sampling, search and selection during inference. Which part explains a gain?

  • Continual parameter learning

    Google Research: The sleep framework, 27 October 2026, research explainer

    How do the researchers measure whether a weight update preserves earlier knowledge?

  • Multimodal knowledge work

    Google DeepMind: MKA technical report, 10 November 2026

    How do visual evidence, multiple files and grading the produced artifact change evaluation?

  • Architecture and inference

    DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash release, 10 September 2026, and the DiffusionGemma model card

    Compare sparse expert routing and block denoising with the basic decoder. Which hardware and inputs were used for the speed measurements?

  • Coordination between agents

    Worse Together, 11 November 2026, Anthropic Fellows research

    Why did a single coordinator do better in the four simulated settings? Try a small scheduling example and score both group success and individual constraints.

  • Evaluation validity and oversight

    OpenAI: Eval-production behaviour gaps, 3 November 2026; CoT monitorability across the model lifecycle, 10 November 2026

    What can a benchmark result or observed trace establish, and what remains unmeasured?

  • Evaluation design

    Anthropic: Building strong safety evaluations, 13 November 2026, engineering report

    Check whether the task setup, grading and uncertainty match the claim being made.

Images and other model designs

Everyone should understand the idea of image patches/vision encoders, joint text–image representations and why OCR alone can miss layout or chart information. A short MIT vision/generative lecture or the chosen multimodal report can support this. Training a vision-language model is optional.

Likewise, learn the purpose of sparse expert routing, long-context methods, recurrent/hybrid alternatives, speculative decoding and text diffusion at an explanatory level. Select one for depth; do not create a second full architecture course inside the core plan.

How to read a paper

For a first pass, identify the question, central claim, comparison and limitation. For a deeper pass, trace the data, objective, baseline, evaluation and compute. For the chosen experiment, change one relevant factor and write down what a small-scale result cannot establish.

Use a one-page note with: claim; mechanism; evidence; confounders; relevance to the project; and the cheapest useful next experiment. Revisit the frontier menu after each substantial project milestone. A new release should replace an older reading when it teaches the same idea better, rather than continually increasing the workload.
