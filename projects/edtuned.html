edTuned

Exploring how agentic AI can make learning support more responsive to individual students.

LLMsLearning agents

The question

How could an intelligent learning system adapt its explanations and learning pathways to the person using it?

My work

  • Explored LLM-based agents for intelligent tutoring, content generation, and personalised learning pathways.
  • Worked on the design of agent workflows and the relationship between student needs and learning content.
  • Considered responsible AI, learner privacy, and appropriate educational use alongside the technical design.
  • Developed the idea through university capstone research and startup exploration with the ThincLab ecosystem.

The takeaway

An educational AI system needs more than useful model outputs. It needs a clear learning purpose, thoughtful interaction design, and a way to evaluate whether it helps the learner.
