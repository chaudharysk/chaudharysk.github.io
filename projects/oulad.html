Data & research

Understanding student success

An investigation into student withdrawal and failure using the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD).

PythonOULADMachine learning

32,593 student records · 10,655,280 interaction logs · 7 linked tables

The question

What patterns in demographic, registration, assessment, and learning activity data help explain student outcomes?

My work

  • Integrated seven related tables and aggregated interaction records into student-level and weekly measures.
  • Built repeatable ingest, cleaning, joining, feature engineering, and modelling stages in Python.
  • Applied data validation, missing-value strategies, deduplication, and cross-table integrity checks.
  • Compared predictive approaches while documenting the transformations and features used.

Research direction

The work explores the factors associated with withdrawal and failure in online courses. A manuscript is in preparation.
