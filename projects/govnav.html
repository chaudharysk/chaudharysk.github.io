GovNav

A team hackathon exploration of AI tools that help people find government services and make sense of regulatory information.

AIService navigation

The question

How can we make the next step clearer when government services, community support, or business regulations are difficult to navigate?

The team project

  • Contributed to Team SPYDER’s GovHack 2025 exploration of service navigation and community AI agents.
  • Explored conversational assistance and plain-language guidance for complex information.
  • Worked within the fast-paced process of hackathon prototyping, collaboration, and communicating an idea.

The context

The repository documents three challenge areas: navigating government services, community agents that bridge service access gaps, and simplifying red tape.
