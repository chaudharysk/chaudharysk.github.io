Published research

Conversational risk analysis

Co-authored research into identifying and classifying potential threats in interpersonal communication, across text and audio.

NLPText & audio

The question

How do classification approaches behave when moving from text-based threat analysis to audio data?

The research

  • Compared machine learning and deep learning methods, including logistic regression, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, LSTM, and RNN.
  • Worked with data preparation, feature engineering, experimentation, and model evaluation.
  • Contributed to research writing and the comparison of approaches across data modalities.

Publication

Bansal, A., Bharadwaj, S., Panchineni, P., Suddala, S. & Chaudhary, S. (2025). Conversational Risk Analysis: Identifying and Classifying Potential Threats in Interpersonal Communication. Congress on Smart Computing Technologies (CSCT 2024), Springer. First published online 1 October 2025.

The linked GitHub repository contains related earlier project work. The Springer chapter is the authoritative source for the published study.
